**[COMPANY NAME]** ("we") uses an internal tool, the **PO Delivery Tracker**, to
track purchase-order line delivery status and to remind the responsible person
before an item is late. This notice explains what personal data the tool holds
about you and why.

## What we collect

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Your name / work email address | Created for you by an administrator |
| Your role (Administrator / Manager / Staff / Viewer) | Set by an administrator |
| Which PO lines are assigned to you | Set when a line is created or edited |
| A record of PO lines you create, edit, or request to delete (with timestamps) | Your actions in the tool |
| Reminder emails sent to you (subject, time) | Generated by the daily reminder job |

We do **not** use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or profiling. The
tool stores only what it needs to do its job.

## Why (lawful basis)

We process this data to run our purchasing operations — assigning delivery
follow-up, sending reminders, and keeping an audit trail of changes. Our lawful
basis is **[legitimate interests / performance of your employment contract —
